Thank you for dropping by! I’m an economist and econometrician, and my research applies causal methods to administrative and survey data. My work centres on three broad themes. The first is migration and political economy: how immigration and legal status shape populist voting, remittances and health decisions. The second is children’s health and development, looking at how intimate partner violence and maternal employment affect the way children fare. The third is institutions and inequality: the long-run welfare legacy of Soviet communism, and how AI is reshaping the public-sector workforce. This page brings that work together in one place – I hope you find something of interest.
